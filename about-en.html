🎯 Goals of the ID

This initiative is designed to create a decentralized cultural identity for every individual, without requiring personal information. We aim to build a network of cultural reformers who can preserve their cultural identity and values without relying on specific institutions, and connect with like-minded people around the world.

  • Build a borderless network of cultural reformers
  • Protect cultural diversity as a shared human asset
  • Connect like-minded people without identity disclosure
  • Promote peace, eliminate violence, foster cultural unity

⚠️ Cultural Threats

Today, local and indigenous cultures face serious threats. Cultural imperialism, intellectual numbing by identity-less media, and AI misuse to manipulate collective identity and memory are among these threats.

  • Imposing foreign values on local communities
  • Mass production of numbing, identity-less content
  • Replacing human decision-making with AI
  • Dividing and polarizing societies

🌐 Local Networks

This project is built on decentralization and handing over control to users. Every group of 50 cultural reformers can form a local network and define their own indicators, rules, and communication channels.

  • Every 50-member group forms a local "cultural hub"
  • Successors and group leaders are chosen by members
  • Gradual transfer of control to top reformers
  • Possibility of forming regional and global networks

✦ Final goal: removal of the initial founder after reaching 10,000 members, giving full control to the community itself

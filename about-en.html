🎯 Project Goals

This project is designed to create a decentralized and voluntary cultural identity for every individual, without requiring personal information. We aim to build a network of cultural reformers who can preserve their cultural identity and values without relying on specific institutions, and connect with like-minded people around the world.

  • Build a borderless network of cultural reformers
  • Protect cultural diversity as a shared human asset
  • Connect like-minded people without identity disclosure
  • Promote peace, eliminate violence, foster cultural unity
⚖️ Fundamental Principles

These unchangeable principles form the core of the project. No one, not even the founder, can alter them.

🔄
No Permanent Ownership
Founder steps down after 10,000 members
🌐
Transition to Decentralization
Temporary management, architecture for handover
🔒
Privacy-First
No identifying data is collected
✅
Voluntary Participation
All activities are optional
🤝
Non-Discrimination
No culture is superior to another
📋
Replicability
Any group can launch their own version
🔍
Transparency
Code is open, but user data remains private
📡 Public Smart Radio-Television

After forming cultural groups (at least 5 people with shared indicators), the group can launch a dedicated Smart Radio-Television channel. This platform serves as a tool for networking, producing, and sharing cultural content.

  • Publish text, audio, images, and video
  • Each group has a dedicated channel
  • Content is produced and managed by the group itself
  • AI suggests content, but does not make human decisions

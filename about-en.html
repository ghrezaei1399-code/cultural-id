📖 About Project

Decentralized Philosophy & Cultural Goals

Why a Cultural Identity Card?

In an era where geographical borders are reinforced but deep human connections are more needed than ever, we need an identity beyond name and nationality. This card is a tool for identifying "cultural kin" based on shared values, not political or economic background.

Our goal is to create a network of cultural reformers who connect with each other based on collective wisdom and human-centric values, independent of institutions.

Core Principles

  • Non-Political & Non-Governmental: Focus solely on cultural heritage and human values.
  • Anonymity: Protecting user privacy is the system's top priority.
  • Decentralized: Data is not stored on any central server to prevent censorship or control.

Theoretical Foundations

This platform is the operational output of over two decades of research in "Civilizational Transition", "Human-Centric AI", and "Invisible Cultural Engineering". The architecture of this system is built upon internationally registered theoretical frameworks aimed at transitioning from the Information Age to the Age of Wisdom.

For studying scientific and theoretical documents:
This project is based on a collection of scientific articles and books registered in international databases (such as Zenodo) on topics such as "Sphere of Wisdom", "Enlightenment Companions", and "Controlled Entanglement". Those interested in the theoretical foundations can access these documents through the author's scientific profile.

Decentralized Philosophy & Cultural Goals

Why a Cultural Identity Card?

In an era where geographical borders are reinforced but deep human connections are more needed than ever, we need an identity beyond name and nationality. This card is a tool for identifying "cultural kin" based on shared values, not political or economic background.

Our goal is to create a network of cultural reformers who connect with each other based on collective wisdom and human-centric values, independent of institutions.

💳 Cultural Identity Card & QR Code

When registering, each member receives a digital card with their unique code. This card includes:

  • Card Code — the member's unique identifier
  • 7 Cultural Values chosen by the member
  • QR Code — for quickly sharing the card
  • Project Slogan — "Preserve cultural diversity; let people find their own cultural commonalities."
🪪
Global Smart Cultural Identity Card
No personal information (name, number, address) is on the card.

🔗 Where Does the QR Code Link To?

The QR code of each member links to their own public card page. This page only includes:

  • The Card Code
  • The 7 chosen cultural values
  • No other personal information

🔒 Note: The QR code is never linked to email, name, or personal information. Anyone who scans the QR code only sees the public cultural card.

  • Connecting with cultural kin worldwide
  • Uploading cultural achievements and displaying them in the global gallery
  • Submitting observations in the "Sphere of Wisdom Laboratory" and receiving three-layer analysis
  • Tracking requests with a tracking code

Core Principles

  • Non-Political & Non-Governmental: Focus solely on cultural heritage and human values.
  • Anonymity: Protecting user privacy is the system's top priority.
  • Decentralized: Data is not stored on any central server to prevent censorship or control.

🔒 Email & Privacy

Providing an email address is completely voluntary and is used only for:

  • Connecting with like-minded individuals who share at least 5 common values
  • Receiving a list of like-minded people
  • Tracking connection requests with a tracking code

🔒 Email is never published anywhere. Emails are only provided to a user when they themselves have requested a connection with like-minded people. In that case, the system automatically finds their like-minded peers and shows the emails only to them. No one else has access to the emails.

🔒 No personal information (name, phone number, address, location) is collected or displayed. Galleries and achievements are completely anonymous.

💡 Note about email: Email is completely voluntary. Each member can provide a real email, a temporary email, or even leave it empty. If someone provides a real email, they accept that it may be seen by others during like-minded connections. Each member can edit their email at any time.

🔎 Track Requests with a Tracking Code

Each connection request (or account deletion request) receives a unique tracking code. By entering this code on the request page, you can:

  • See your own email
  • See the number of like-minded peers (those who share at least 5 common values with you)
  • Track the status of your request

💡 Note: The tracking box is located on the Persian and English request pages. Only the person who has the tracking code can see the result.

From Like-Minded to Co-Builders
When a like-minded cluster reaches 50 members, that cluster can request the site's dedicated version guide from the admin. Then the cluster can:
  • 🌐 Launch its own independent site with its own name and brand.
  • 📍 Develop its cultural network in its own region or language.
  • 👥 Attract new members from its own community.
  • 📡 Publish its cultural content on the Smart Radio & TV.
This cycle continues. Each 50-member cluster creates a new site and a new network.
This is how the project grows beyond 10,000 members and reaches hundreds of thousands.

🧠 Introduction to the Sphere of Wisdom

The Sphere of Wisdom is a theoretical and operational framework for cultural observation, analysis, and action. This framework is based on the belief that human, social, cultural, and civilizational phenomena cannot be understood solely through a single discipline, specialty, or sphere.

«Better seeing, before better judging.»

🌟 Why Do We Need the Sphere of Wisdom?

In today's complex world, many cultural and social problems cannot be solved with one-dimensional approaches. We often fail to see hidden capacities, fail to identify real barriers, ignore gaps between spheres, and judge before observing.

The Sphere of Wisdom gives us tools to observe (not interpret), distinguish (not judge), discover capacities (not just see problems), and make human dignity the basis of evaluation.

📋 Key Principles

🔍

Reality-Centered

All analyses are based on real phenomena.

👁️

💎

🛠️ How to Use This Framework?

  1. Go to the Sphere of Wisdom Community
  2. Submit a raw observation of a phenomenon
  3. The system or admin will generate a cluster and action guide for you
  4. Implement the suggested actions at individual, network, or policy level
  5. Submit your emergence achievement and share it in the gallery

📌 Practical Examples

Example 1: Loss of Traditional Skills
Raw Observation: "In our neighborhood, young people have forgotten traditional skills and there is no space for learning them."
Cluster: Human / Knowledge
Suggested Action: Form local educational groups with elder participation
Example 2: Water Resource Management
Raw Observation: "Despite sufficient water resources in the region, farmers' access to water is not properly managed and cultivated area decreases each year."
Cluster: Survival & Future / Governance
Suggested Action: Form a local network for participatory water management
